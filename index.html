🏀 Basketball Academy Planner

Youth basketball academies across Europe

Basketball academies for kids aged 8–18, in dense family neighbourhoods near schools, so getting to training after school is a short walk or bus ride. Each academy also has a CrossFit-style fitness zone (rigs, barbells, dumbbells, rowers, bikes) for parents, adults and teen athletes. Adult sessions, pickup games and camps fill the court hours the kids aren't using.

At a glance

The plan

🎯 Who it's for

Kids aged 8–18 in age groups (8–11 fundamentals, 12–14 development, 15–18 competitive). Adults use the court and fitness zone in the evenings and at weekends.

🏀 Why basketball

One court and one coach can run 20–40 kids at once, far more per coach than racket sports. See Why basketball.

📍 Where it goes

Dense residential areas within walking distance of several schools. See Best cities.

💶 How it makes money

Kids coaching is the core income. Fitness memberships, camps, adult training, pickup games, court rental, parties and sponsors add to it. See Revenue streams.

🇪🇺 How it's funded

Founder capital, plus EU (Erasmus+ Sport) and national sport grants through a non-profit club partner. See EU funding.

Why basketball?

Give five sports the same floor space and compare them. Basketball serves the most kids per coach, and coaches are an academy's biggest ongoing cost. One court and one coach can run a session for 20–40 kids.

Head-to-head

Full comparison

The honest picture. Basketball wins clearly on kids per coach and coach cost per kid. Staff costs never stop, while a fit-out is paid once. Squash fits slightly more kids into the same floor space, but it needs one coach per court, so 5–6× the coaches. Badminton and pickleball courts are cheaper to fit out, but every court still needs its own coach, and badminton needs a 9 m ceiling. Padel uses the most space per player of all five.

Other reasons basketball fits a kids' academy

👥 Team sport = kids stay

Kids come back for their friends and their team. Retention is usually higher than in individual sports.

🏫 Schools already play it

Basketball is taught in PE across Europe. That makes school partnerships and recruitment easier.

🎒 Cheap to start for families

Just trainers and a ball. No racket, no court booking, so it's easier for parents to say yes.

🔁 One court, many uses

The same floor hosts camps, 3×3, pickup games, adult leagues, events, and other sports (futsal, volleyball) in quiet hours.

📈 Growing in Europe

3×3 is an Olympic sport, Europe has NBA stars (Wembanyama, Jokić, Dončić, Antetokounmpo), and the NBA is planning a European league.

⚖️ Boys and girls together

Works for both, with plenty of grants for girls' participation.

EU funding

Grants and finance available to a youth basketball academy in the EU, with amounts. Checked 14 October 2026.

Read this first. Most EU and national sport grants go to non-profit organisations and pay for projects (a programme, an event, a coach exchange), not day-to-day running costs. The usual setup is a company that runs the academy plus a registered non-profit club or association that applies for grants. Erasmus+ also requires partner organisations in other countries, which suits a multi-city academy.
Timing. The 2026 Erasmus+ Sport deadline was 5 March 2026 and has passed. Plan for the 2027 round, which is normally announced in autumn with an early-spring deadline.

    Choosing the exact location inside a city

    The city score only tells you where to start looking. The real decision is the neighbourhood, because "near schools" and "near homes" only really count at street level. Check for:

    🏫 Near schools

    3+ primary or secondary schools within 1 km (a 10–15 min walk). Kids can come straight from school, and the schools are where you recruit.

    🏘️ In residential areas

    Dense family housing, not business districts. Check census data for the share of households with children aged 5–17.

    🚌 Easy to reach

    Bus, tram or metro stop within 5 minutes, safe cycling routes, and a short-stay drop-off area for parents.

    📐 The building

    At least 7 m clear height for the court and roughly 1,000 m² of floor (see the Calculator). Warehouses and light-industrial units are usually cheapest.

    ⚔️ Competition

    Map basketball clubs within 3 km. Long waiting lists at nearby clubs mean there's demand they can't meet.

    💶 Ability to pay

    Mid-level household incomes keep fees affordable. Add grant-funded free places in low-income areas.
