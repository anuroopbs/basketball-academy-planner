Our mission

Get every kid in Europe playing by turning empty courts, school halls and community spaces into busy youth-sport hubs.

Start with one warehouse hub that trains coaches and keeps its court busy from 06:00 to 22:00. Then run our programmes in the schools and community centres around it on a profit share. Then repeat it city by city across Europe.

🧠 Expert review: what to validate before investing 6 checks
1 · Pilot before the lease

Run 8–12 weeks of paid sessions in hired school halls. Measure sign-ups, attendance and churn, which turns occupancy guesses into data.

2 · Find the building first

Rent per m² and a clear height of 7 m+ decide whether the hub works. Put 3 real warehouse quotes into the calculator.

3 · Lock in coaches early

Coaches are the product. Check local rules (France: state diploma + card; Ireland: Garda vetting).

4 · One school letter of intent

A signed letter of intent from one school or council proves the partner model to investors and grant bodies.

5 · Non-profit arm from day one

Most grants need a non-profit club. Register it now so you can apply in the 2027 Erasmus+ round.

6 · Biggest risks

Year-1 occupancy, rent, coach turnover, venues cancelling, safeguarding. Mitigate with a pilot, profit-share deals, a coach academy and vetting.
